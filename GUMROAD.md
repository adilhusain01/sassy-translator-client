# ✨ Sassy - Add Attitude to Your Text

Transform any boring text into sassy, witty responses with just a right-click! Sassy is a Chrome extension that adds personality and flair to your writing, perfect for social media, messages, or whenever you need that extra spark of attitude.

## 🌟 Features

- **Three Sass Levels**
  - 😊 **Subtle Chuckle**: Light humor with a playful nudge
  - 😏 **Witty Remark**: Smart, snappy, and full of personality
  - 💅 **Full-Blown Sass**: Maximum attitude in minimal words

- **Seamless Integration**
  - Works on any webpage
  - Simple right-click operation
  - Beautiful, non-intrusive overlay design

- **Customizable Experience**
  - Choose your preferred sass level
  - Enable/disable with one click
  - Powered by Gemini AI for creative responses

## ⚡️ How It Works

1. Select any text on any webpage
2. Right-click and choose "Make it Sassy"
3. Watch as your text transforms into a witty masterpiece!
